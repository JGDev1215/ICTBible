The Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm

Core Definition

"The daily range structure can be really broken down and simplified with it starts with a price equilibrium that's Asian range then there's a manipulation and that's always going to come by way of some news event some news driver either at the time of the manipulation or just before it that's the Judah swing" (15:04)

The Algorithm Sequence

Algorithm Rules

The Impossible Sequences

"The main thing is I want you to understand is is it never goes consolidation retracement it never does consolidation reversal it's always consolidation expansion and then from expansion it goes either retracement or reversal" (18:35)

Expansion First, Always

"Nothing can happen until consolidation consolidation is when the Market's quiet why why is that important because that's when the orders are building up in the marketplace the market makers will allow orders to build up above and below the range the next stage is always expansion" (17:26)

The Choice After Expansion

"Once we're in the expansion stage okay then you have a choice it can retrace come back to the order blocket just left behind and then recapitalize that and then make another leg up or down relative to the direction it's moved or once it's moved into expansion it can reverse" (18:09)

Daily Range Structure

Time-Based Framework

"Every day starts with consolidation Asian range after midnight there's a manipulation that takes place that's expansion it's coming in form of a Judah swing what is it doing it's making the higher low in London that's the London swing for a reversal that's a run on stops" (13:57)

Daily Range on Buy Day

"We're going to use the perspective it's a buy day that means that Asia the Asian range had a small consolidation and then right after midnight New York time there's a drop down in price that's the manipulation making the false move for the low there's a range expansion then it goes into to the reversal that's classic London open scenario where it shoots down runs the stops and then what happens it expands again" (15:43)

Key Time Windows

5:00 AM to 8:00 AM Consolidation

"Between 5:00 in the morning to 8:00 in the morning in that time window the market will go back into consolidation then it'll have a retracement between 8:00 and 8:30 in the morning New York time" (16:16)

London Close Reversal

"The range will expand and make the rest of the day going up into 10:00 or 11:00 in the morning New York time where it will have a reversal again that's London close then the market will go into consolidation ending true day" (16:34)

Price Process Rules

The Generic Systematic Process

"When you understand this algorithm the way it moves and way it operates it's very generic it's very systematic it has only a few options to go to and they're time sensitive they're day sensitive okay they're intermarket related" (19:13)

Weekly Range Application

"That same thing happens with a weekly range the weekly range is the same thing Sunday's open consolidation then there's an expansion move in Monday then what there's a reversal on Tuesday or or Monday and then what there's another expansion move then it goes back in consolidation midweek and then it's either going to do what it's going to reverse or it's going to retrace" (24:04)

Identifying the Next Move

"When you get to the the level of month 8 and month nine in the mentorship you're going to be so strong at knowing what the next thing's going to happen in your in your price that you're studying you'll know all these four things okay there's only certain processes that have to take place in a certain order" (20:12)

Repeating Characteristics
