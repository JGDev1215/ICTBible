Impulse Price Swings & Market Protraction

Very similar ideas but uniquely different

Definitions Market Protraction Three Primary Moves Time Elements Trading Application Key Difference Study & Review

Impulse Price Swings - Definition

"when you look at price action you need to be thinking in terms of impulse price swings because inside the impulse price swings it's going to give you a lot of detail"

"we have price swings moving from high down to a low to another high down to another low to a high to a low to another high making another low up to a high down to a low up to a high down to a low"

"there are smaller more specific impulse price swings that have a lot more influence over the marketplace in the form of a manipulative move or market making manipulation"

Market Protraction - Definition

"market protraction is time sensitive it's an impulse price swing that is highly sensitive to a time of day"

"we can look at time sensitive impulse price swings which is market protraction"

"there's usually a protractionary market phase that enters the marketplace and it's a small little impulse price swing that's counter the major direction that you're going to see after that specific time of day"
