Sick Sister Concept

"The Sick Sister approach or concept that I'm using is that it's going to try to do it later on and you want to put your focus on the weakest one"

Consolidation Context

"The economic calendar this week is heavy-handed on the Wednesday Thursday and Friday so Monday and Tuesday is lacking any real meaningful medium or high impact news drivers so it's expected to have these consolidation dates which is what we're seeing here"

(0:36-0:54)
Converging New Week Opening Gaps

"Whenever you have converging new week opening gaps or real close to one another the Market's going to be real likely to consolidate and go sideways"

"That doesn't mean it can't be traded or you can't find setups in it it just means you have to be much more Nimble and you have to be more versed in Market symmetry"

SMT vs Sick Sister

SMT (Smart Money Technique)

"This high is higher than this one whereas this high is higher we have a very specific time of day and we're also looking at this Divergence"

Sick Sister Approach

"The Sick Sister approach or concept that I'm using is that it's going to try to do it later on and you want to put your focus on the weakest one"

Key Distinction

"It's similar but the approach to using the divergences and the later discount or later premium how you use the information is starkly different"

Identifying the Sick Sister

"Notice that this Market here on the Dow was able to make a higher high whereas the NASDAQ was unwilling to make that higher high"

(1:58-2:11)
  • "Two of both highs were taken out on the Dow"
  • "One of two highs were taken out on ES"
  • "Neither high was taken out on NASDAQ - that's the Sick Sister"
The Weak Market

"This one couldn't even muster up enough momentum to climb higher"

"Using the Sick Sister in a consolidation Market means we're looking for the one that failed to make a higher high or lower low which is like typical SMT"

(7:38-7:47)

Entry Framework

"If this one's weak we're going to wait for this one to drop down into a deep discount"
