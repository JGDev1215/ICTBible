Core Concept

SMT — Smart Money Tool / Smart Money Technique

4:01

"See that lower low when S&P was saying I don't want to go that low — so it was resisting going lower. Then we retraced higher just like this NASDAQ did, but that NASDAQ made a lower low than that of 10 o'clock in the morning, while at the same time the S&P said no — once again I'm not going lower than the low I made at 10 o'clock. So we had a divergence here and here. No indicators folks — I'm using intermarket relationships and this is what I dub SMT — Smart Money Technique or Smart Money Tool."

SMT Defined

"No indicators folks — I'm using intermarket relationships and this is what I dub SMT — Smart Money Technique or Smart Money Tool." When NASDAQ makes a lower low while S&P does not (or vice versa), this divergence is a confirmation signal aligned with the existing bias.

SMT Divergence — NQ makes lower low, ES does not — bullish signal (4:01–4:27)
4:39

"The importance of that is I already had a bias that's bullish. There was no short trades that were lined up with this model today. There was no interest in wanting to be short. I want to focus on being long."

Model Filter

Why the Short Did Not Qualify

5:04

"Yes you can see there is a rally up, it breaks the short-term low, a fair value gap, it trades up into it and breaks down — big deal. This doesn't fit the criteria because the displacement leg here would require us to see a fair value gap above 50% of the high and the low — the displacement leg — and we don't have that."

Displacement Leg Requirement for a Short

The fair value gap created within the displacement leg must appear "above 50% of the high and the low — the displacement leg." If the FVG falls in the lower half of the displacement leg, the criteria is not met.

5:24

"Nothing here fits the model for a short."

Concept

Close Proximity Entries

Close Proximity Entry Defined

"I kind of like want to touch a little bit tonight with close proximity entries — that means you might not get so precise with your entries, you might fumble with your order entry or something might happen."

7:35

"What do you do in this situation if you miss the ideal entry? Well, as long as you're getting in there really close or like I'm suggesting here — a close proximity entry — I don't think there's anything wrong with it."

11:03

"This was not entering in the fair value gap — it was in close proximity to it. So the underlying idea was it was going down in here to go higher, retrace into that to go higher."

Entry Levels from the Trade (E-mini S&P)

First entry: 3994.50 — close proximity to FVG. Heat against: "less than two handles." Second entry: 3994.50 — added near secondary FVG after market structure break higher. Heat: "less than one handle." Exit: 4022.50.

Concept

Adding a Second Position

8:32

"When the market starts to rally I have one more indication that the market structure has shifted again bullish with these highs taken out here — and then they had a small little fair value gap out there and then you watch me add more. The limit order came in, filled it."

Condition for Adding

Market structure breaks higher (highs taken out) during the rally → secondary FVG forms → add a second position there. The entry is on the limit order into that secondary FVG as price retraces.

Calendar Rule

Day Before a Major News Event

3:14

"We're in a day before Fed chair speaks — so there's going to be very low volatility as a result of that, so it's going to be a small range day. Doesn't mean you can't trade it, obviously, but it takes a little bit more experience."

3:30

"I'm not saying that you should trade ahead of Fed chair Powell speaking or any future Fed chairman because it's going to be a very quiet day usually like this. And it's important to know when the volatility is likely to occur and when also the volatility is likely to shrink up and get kind of like lethargic."

17:19

"Tomorrow's going to be very volatile — be careful. Do not try to trade tomorrow, you'll probably hurt yourself if you do."

  • 01 Day before a major news event (Fed chair, major data release): expect a small range, lethargic session with low volatility.
  • 02 Day of the major event: "Tomorrow's going to be very volatile — be careful. Do not try to trade tomorrow."
  • 03 This episode: Tuesday May 17 had 8:30 AM retail sales AND 2:00 PM Fed chair speaking. "There's going to be all kinds of volatility at two o'clock tomorrow."
