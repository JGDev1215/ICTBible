Journal Entry Rules

Phrase entries as if you saw it in advance: "phrase it in such a way like you saw this in advance"

"never try to put anything negative in it don't say i wish i would have saw this or i was foolish and i didn't see this unfold"

Review cadence: "each weekend looking at the previous week looking at the previous month and just scrolling through your charts and reading your annotations"
