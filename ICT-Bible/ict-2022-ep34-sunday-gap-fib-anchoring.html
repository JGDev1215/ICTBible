Fibonacci Anchoring — Anchor to the Impulsive Swing in Your Trade Direction
◈

The rule stated explicitly: "you're looking at expansion swings okay or impulsive price swings in the direction you're trying to trade you don't want to use a price leg from high to low measuring something that you're trying to go long on"

Hunting a Long

Anchor Fib to the impulsive up leg (Low → High). Retracement below 50% = discount. Buy FVG in that discount zone.

Hunting a Short

Anchor Fib to the impulsive down leg (High → Low). Retracement above 50% = premium. Sell FVG in that premium zone.

What NOT to Do

"they will look at a range like this... and they'll measure that range low to high to find the 50 level and then use that over here for a discount that's not how we do it"
