Inefficiency Behavior — The Primary Signal

100:35
Inefficiency Behavior Rule Price should enter, touch, and repel quickly. One pass. Not camp inside.
101:16
inefficiencies or gaps you want them to go in stick your toe in the water and then run away you just want to have one quick high five moment and you're out of there

102:00
Analogy: the more times a trend line is touched the stronger it is — same logic here is wrong. More touches = going through it.
103:10
Mechanics behind it: shorts accumulated at open drop, first retracement = OTE for shorts. Relative equal lows form → retail sees support → they buy. Puts sell stops below lows for smart money to use later.
104:06

104:36

105:29

106:11

107:00 – 115:00

Stop Loss Rule on High Resistance Days

107:22
HR Stop Rule Stop loss = entry FVG width × 2 (two times the FVG height above the entry). Accounts for deeper retracements characteristic of HR conditions.
109:17
LR Stop Rule Low resistance day: stop = one tick above consequent encroachment of the FVG. Much tighter.
110:20
Leverage Rule Push maximum (not over) leverage on LR days. Do NOT use high leverage on HR days.
110:43
Teaching approach: always take partials when it takes out a low. Over time this trains pattern recognition of where targets actually are vs where you thought.
111:04
On a 10-contract position in HR conditions: take 6 contracts off when it first breaks below a low. Remaining 4 stay for deeper target.
112:00 – 125:00

Manual Intervention — The Mechanics

112:49
HR Signature #4 Spiky runs outside of inefficiencies. Not normal buy/sell pressure. Someone manually running stops.
113:00
LR Precision Signal On LR days: market delivers to the tip. When precision is absent = manual intervention confirmed.
113:32
Manual intervention at the second run up: price sat at that level. Afforded the open interest to enter. Look at time and sales — contracts traded heavily there = smart money slipped in.
114:59
After manipulation: shorts buy back at low price. Retail longs have sell stops below that low (protection). Market runs through = smart money exits shorts, retail stops fill them on the other side.
117:50
LR = Algorithmic Low resistance = AI driven → fluid → runs right to liquidity to the tick.
HR = Manual High resistance = someone overriding the algorithm → disrupting order flow.
120:37
Rule Losing precision on PD arrays = truest sign of manual intervention. Not: algorithm changed. Not: model broken.
"when you lose the Precision elements of my PD arrays you're seeing the truest indication that there is manual intervention underway"
121:00

123:54

132:38
Turtle Soup in HR If bearish in HR conditions → look for turtle soup sells. Micro shifts in market structure that look bullish but are just them taking buy stops to accumulate short positions.
133:42
Pattern: high → runs buy stops → sells off → high → runs stops → sells off = characteristic HR market signature.
134:54

135:08
