Stop Loss Management

Initial stop — placed at mean threshold of bullish order block:

"Your stop was below the middle of this candle — that's the mean threshold of the bullish order block. So I don't think it's going to go down below that once I'm entering."

25:03 – 25:11

After price trades above the order block's high — stop must still stay below the candle's low:

"My stop-loss must remain below this candle's low — because it can dig into that candle — because it's going to act as an order block."

25:16 – 25:23

Premature stop placement — the mistake to avoid:

"If I put my stop loss inside this area here — I'm stopped out prematurely. Chances are you probably wouldn't have the wherewithal to get back in."

25:28 – 25:37

When to raise the stop — after price takes out the next set of down closed candles:

"The market rallies above — takes out these two down closed candles — now the stop can be raised from whatever would be below this down close candle — because the idea is these closed candles are one order block that should not be violated if it's bullish."

26:03 – 26:21

Invalidation — if stop is taken:

"If it comes down and breaks the low of these down closed candles — well there you go. You probably did the right thing by getting stopped out because it might be failing and going lower."

Pyramiding — Position Sizing Structure

Three entries treated as one trade — largest size first, decreasing with each add.

"I'm pyramiding — I'm building the biggest position initially and then every time I buy in again I'm building it with a smaller position than I had prior to when I'm entering now — because I have all the equity behind that entry with three supporting the two I'm buying here."
