ICT Bearish Breaker

What a Bearish Breaker Is — and Is Not

"this swing low here to a higher high than that of here — is a breaker — okay that's my ict bearish breaker — what is a breaker — a breaker is a pattern where price runs out a pool of liquidity — in this case is a buy side liquidity pool — and then it reverses and trades back down below that short-term low — if it trades back up to that and the narrative is bearish"

20:20–20:44

"notice that it's not just simply we have this pattern here and that's all it is — no — you must know what the market's likely to be reaching for — if you leave that out — it's not a breaker — it's a gamble — it's a guess — it's a speculation — that's aimless"

20:44–21:03

Bearish breaker: swing low → higher high (BSL run) → reverses below swing low → retrace to breaker → continuation lower — ONLY valid when narrative is bearish and market has a known target

"this down closed candle the lowest one prior to move up clearing that short-term high — this is the bearish order block — and i level that would expect and anticipate that level repelling price and go lower"
