How Narrative is Applied Here

ICT uses narrative to explain why a price dip below the FVG low does not invalidate the setup: "It does not negate the fair value Gap because I'm using narrative." The narrative frames where retail stop losses are resting and therefore where price is likely to reach before continuing.

Practical Rules

Trading the Open (9:30 Equities)

  • 01 "When you're entering on like the opening like 9:30 equities opening uh you could take a long entry here but you don't know how far it's going to whip below that low and you could be wrong and painfully wrong."
  • 02 "If you're trading the open like this, stops can be taken really really easily and then reverse and then never give you a chance to be in it."
  • 03 On stop placement at the open: "I'm allowing myself a little bit of flexibility… I'm not trying to encourage you to trade without a stop loss. Believe me, I'm right here ready to click and close it if it trades below it."
  • 04 On when to move stop to entry: "At this moment I could put the stop loss on at my entry and if it takes me out then I would basically move to the sidelines and wouldn't take any more trades today."
