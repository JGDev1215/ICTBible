The Draw on Liquidity Doesn't Need to Be Reached

"the draw on liquidity does not need to be traded to to have a profitable framework or setup okay so what do i mean by that well if we're below this objective and i'm thinking that market's going to draw up to that level whether it be today or into next week you look for a setup that will allow you some kind of participation in this expansion towards that it doesn't need to get there as you'll soon see"

2:05
◆

The draw on liquidity (relative equal highs, old highs) acts as directional context — it gives you the reason to participate. The trade doesn't require price to actually reach that level to be profitable.

Sequence: sell side taken (ran below REQ lows) → price comes back above REQ lows → next draw is buy side (relative equal highs above). This is the same premium-to-discount / discount-to-premium logic applied to intraday structure.

Self-Talk & the Reticular Activating System

"you're pouring yourself into the chart with your annotations for back testing and you're doing it in such a way where you're tricking your subconscious like you expected this to happen"

17:55

"over time when you do that you're recording this subconsciously you don't realize you're doing it but by doing these things over and over and over again it activates your reticular activating system it's the same thing happens when you buy a car you go out the next day all of a sudden it seems like everybody else bought the same car the only difference is is you've been more sensitive to that because it's more meaningful to you so these charts will take on that same characteristic when you make them more meaningful to you"

18:06

"how do you do that you place your personality and your interest in the chart so everything here i'm giving you a narrative here you would do the same thing just typing it out putting it in there and then screen capturing it and storing it in your journal or printing it out and putting in your journal"

18:32

18:50

"critics that hear me say this you're like oh you're telling you to lie to yourself i'm being honest you are lying in the chart when you do your annotations for study purposes you're tricking your brain like you had experience seeing this happen but because over time you're doing that your subconscious will retain that and when you start seeing it live it will remember hey i remember that because i did that before when you technically really didn't but then now you have pseudo experience"

18:57
◈

The mechanism: "it activates your reticular activating system" — the brain filters for what is made meaningful to you. Annotating charts as if you foresaw the move programs your subconscious to recognize the pattern live.

How to Back Test

"what is back testing going back and looking at moves like this okay marking them up on your chart now you can do it either using the replay button watching them paint partially or you can go back in hindsight and mark up your chart like this this is all back testing"
